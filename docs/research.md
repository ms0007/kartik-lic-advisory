## 3. Official LIC Riders Research & Verified UINs

Riders are optional endorsements that can be attached to base policies at inception (or anniversary if permitted by plan rules) upon payment of additional rider premium:

1. **LIC's Accidental Death and Disability Benefit Rider**
   - **UIN:** 512B209V02
   - **Cover:** Additional accidental sum assured upon accidental demise; monthly installment benefit and waiver of future rider premiums in the event of permanent and total disability resulting from an accident.
   - **Safe to publish:** Yes, with clear note that eligibility criteria and medical caps apply.

2. **LIC's Accident Benefit Rider**
   - **UIN:** 512B203V03
   - **Cover:** Lump-sum accidental benefit paid if death occurs as a result of an accident within 180 days.
   - **Safe to publish:** Yes.

3. **LIC's Premium Waiver Benefit (PWB) Rider**
   - **UIN:** 512B204V04
   - **Cover:** Waives future premiums of base policy in case of untimely demise of the proposer (frequently attached to child plans such as Amritbaal or Jeevan Lakshya).
   - **Safe to publish:** Yes.

4. **LIC's New Term Assurance Rider**
   - **UIN:** 512B210V02
   - **Cover:** Provides additional pure risk term cover equal to or up to the basic sum assured on the death of the life assured.
   - **Safe to publish:** Yes.

5. **LIC's Critical Illness Health Rider**
   - **Cover:** Lump-sum critical illness benefit upon confirmed first diagnosis of specified covered illnesses (e.g. 15 or 40 conditions options).
   - **Safe to publish:** Yes, highlighting that strict diagnosis definitions, waiting periods (typically 90 days), and survival periods (typically 30 days) govern eligibility.
